隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

Chinese Poems - 古诗成语学中文（"本App"）

最后更新日期 / Last updated: 2026年8月31日 (August 31, 2026)

1. 我们不收集你的个人数据

Chinese Poems - 古诗成语学中文 是一款专为海外华裔儿童设计的中文学习应用（古诗、成语、笔顺跟读等）。 本App不会收集、存储或分享任何用户个人身份信息，包括但不限于姓名、邮箱、位置信息、联系人或设备标识符。

Chinese Poems is a Chinese-learning app (classical poems, idioms, stroke-order practice) designed for overseas Chinese children. This app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. 本地存储的数据

App 会将你的学习进度（例如已完成的古诗、成语、打卡记录）保存在你设备本地，用于在你下次打开 App 时继续学习。 这些数据不会上传到我们的服务器，也不会被用于广告或第三方分析。

The app stores your learning progress (e.g. completed poems, idioms, check-in streaks) locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is never uploaded to our servers and is never used for advertising or third-party analytics.

3. 订阅与支付信息

如果你购买应用内自动续期订阅，支付交易由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，我们不会接触或存储你的支付卡信息。 订阅的管理、续费与取消均通过你的 Apple ID 账户设置完成，具体请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

If you purchase an auto-renewable subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Subscriptions are managed, renewed, and cancelled through your Apple ID account settings — see Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

4. 儿童隐私

本App面向包含儿童在内的家庭用户，我们始终将儿童隐私保护放在首位。由于本App不收集任何个人数据， 因此不存在向儿童收集、使用或披露个人信息的情形。

This app is intended for family use, including by children, and we take children's privacy seriously. Because the app collects no personal data whatsoever, there is no collection, use, or disclosure of children's personal information of any kind.

5. 第三方服务

本App 使用 Apple 提供的标准系统服务（例如 App Store 内购、本地通知），这些服务由 Apple 按其自身隐私政策运作。 本App 本身不嵌入任何第三方广告 SDK 或数据分析工具。

This app relies only on standard system services provided by Apple (e.g. In-App Purchase, local notifications), which operate under Apple's own privacy policy. The app itself does not embed any third-party advertising SDK or analytics tool.

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
