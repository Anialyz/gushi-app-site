Chinese Poems - 古诗成语学中文（"本App"）

1. 数据处理概述 / How Data Is Handled

Chinese Poems - 古诗成语学中文 是一款专为海外华裔儿童设计的中文学习应用（古诗、成语、笔顺跟读等）。 你无需向我们提交姓名、邮箱或联系人等个人资料。学习进度保留在设备上；订阅与广告服务的数据处理方式见下文。

Chinese Poems is a Chinese-learning app (classical poems, idioms, stroke-order practice) designed for overseas Chinese children. You do not need to provide us with personal details such as your name, email address, or contacts. Learning progress remains on the device; subscription and advertising data handling is described below.

2. 本地存储的数据

App 会将你的学习进度（例如已完成的古诗、成语、打卡记录）保存在你设备本地，用于在你下次打开 App 时继续学习。 这些数据不会上传到我们的服务器，也不会提供给 Google 用于广告。

The app stores your learning progress (e.g. completed poems, idioms, check-in streaks) locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. Learning progress remains on the device and is not provided to Google for advertising.

3. 订阅与支付信息

如果你购买应用内自动续期订阅，支付交易由 Apple 的 App Store 处理，我们不会接触或存储你的支付卡信息。 订阅的管理、续费与取消均通过你的 Apple ID 账户设置完成，具体请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

If you purchase an auto-renewable subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Subscriptions are managed, renewed, and cancelled through your Apple ID account settings — see Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

4. 儿童隐私

本App面向包含儿童在内的家庭用户，我们始终将儿童隐私保护放在首位。Google Mobile Ads 的广告请求会被配置为面向儿童的处理方式， 并设置最高为 G 级的内容分级。本App不会为了展示这些情境式广告而请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限。

This app is intended for family use, including by children, and we take children's privacy seriously. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment and a maximum G content rating. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission for these contextual ads.

5. 广告与第三方服务 / Advertising and Third-Party Services

Chinese Poems 使用 Google Mobile Ads，在符合条件的免费用户完成一次学习活动后展示有限的非个性化插页式广告。 App 会将广告请求配置为面向儿童的处理方式，并设置最高为 G 级的内容分级。App 不会为了这些情境式广告请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限。 Google 可能会根据其政策处理设备信息、诊断信息、从网络数据推断的粗略位置以及广告互动数据。订阅用户不会加载或看到广告，学习进度仍保留在设备上。 详情请参阅 Google 隐私权政策。

Chinese Poems uses Google Mobile Ads to show limited, non-personalized interstitial ads to eligible free users after a completed learning activity. The app configures ad requests for child-directed treatment and a maximum G content rating. It does not request App Tracking Transparency permission for these contextual ads. Google may process device information, diagnostics, coarse location derived from network data, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies. Subscribers do not load or see ads. Learning progress remains on the device. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

本App 还使用 Apple 提供的标准系统服务（例如 App Store 内购、本地通知），这些服务由 Apple 按其自身隐私政策运作。

The app also uses standard system services provided by Apple (such as In-App Purchase and local notifications), which operate under Apple's own privacy policy.

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
