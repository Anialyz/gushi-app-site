1. 运营者与适用范围 / Operator and scope

本政策适用于 VITANIA PTE. LTD.（新加坡）提供的观舌日记。本版本无需注册，不使用 Supabase 账户服务，订阅由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 管理；部分 Android 版本可展示广告，具体见下方广告说明；iOS 版本不展示广告。应用提供舌象观察和健康教育，不提供医学诊断、处方或紧急医疗服务。

This policy covers Guanshe Diary, provided by VITANIA PTE. LTD., Singapore. This version requires no account, uses no Supabase account service, and uses Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Some Android versions may display ads as described below; the iOS version has no ads. It provides tongue observations and wellness education, not medical diagnosis, prescriptions or emergency care.

2. 照片与第三方 AI / Photos and third-party AI

照片分析的第三方提供商为 DeepSeek（Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.）。在你明确同意后，照片和界面语言经 VITANIA 在 Render（美国俄勒冈地区）托管的服务器发送给 DeepSeek，用于描述照片中可见的外观；服务器不会自动切换到其他模型提供商。照片可能包含健康、面部或其他个人信息。身体感受问卷、联系方式、订阅凭据和原文件名不发送给 DeepSeek。

The third-party photo-analysis provider is DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.). After your explicit permission, the photo and interface language pass through VITANIA’s server hosted on Render in Oregon, United States, to DeepSeek for descriptions of visible appearance. The server does not automatically switch to another model provider. Photos may contain health, facial or other personal information. Check-in answers, contact details, subscription proofs and the original filename are not sent to DeepSeek.

157 及以后包含本机照片检查流程：选择照片不会立即上传。你先在本机裁剪并查看将要上传的副本，再逐张勾选同意和点击确认。副本重新编码为 JPEG，最长边不超过 1024 像素，清除原文件嵌入的位置、拍摄时间和设备元数据。裁剪并不等于匿名化，照片中可见的人脸、文字及健康信息仍可能存在。取消会清除本次应用内待上传照片，不删除系统相册原件。

Build 157 and later include on-device photo review: selection does not immediately upload a photo. First crop and inspect the upload copy on your device, then acknowledge and confirm each upload. The copy is re-encoded as JPEG with a maximum dimension of 1024 pixels, omitting the original file’s embedded location, capture time and device metadata. Cropping is not anonymisation; visible faces, text and health information may remain. Cancellation clears the pending in-app photo, not the original in your photo library.

拒绝上传不影响手动记录及一般科普。你可撤回未来上传许可；应用会取消尚未发出的待处理请求，但无法收回已经传送的数据。未提供所需明确授权的旧客户端请求会被拒绝，请更新应用。模型自由文本不会作为疾病诊断、健康评分或个体用药建议展示。

Declining upload does not prevent manual records or general education. You can withdraw permission for future uploads; the app cancels pending requests that have not been sent, but cannot recall data already transmitted. Requests from older clients without the required explicit consent are rejected; update the app. Model-authored free text is not presented as a diagnosis, health score or personalised medication advice.

3. 问卷、本地记录与权限 / Questionnaire, local data and permissions

当前版本的症状选择、问卷和报告显示保存在本次应用会话内，问卷答案不包含在照片分析请求中。界面语言保存在设备本地。应用仅使用你主动选择或拍摄的照片，不读取通讯录、精确位置或健康数据库。系统相机权限可在设备设置中管理。关闭并重新启动应用可结束本次会话；清除网站数据或卸载应用可移除本地偏好，系统备份的处理取决于你的设备设置。

In this version, symptom selections, questionnaire answers and displayed reports are held in the current app session; questionnaire answers are not included in photo-analysis requests. The language preference is stored locally. The app uses only photos you select or take and does not read your address book, precise location or health database. Manage camera permissions in device settings. Fully closing and restarting the app ends the session; clearing website data or uninstalling removes local preferences, subject to your device’s backup settings.

4. 保存、用途与跨境处理 / Retention, uses and international processing

我们的分析应用不建立照片或报告的持久化数据库，不主动将照片、报告或模型原始响应写入应用日志，也不使用照片训练自己的模型或进行广告定向。请求处理中照片会暂存在服务器内存；分析响应设置为不缓存。应用关闭后结束本次内存会话，但你自行导出的报告、系统相册和设备备份不会因此被删除。

Our analysis application does not maintain a persistent photo or report database, intentionally log photos, reports or raw model responses, or use photos to train our own models or target advertising. Photos are held temporarily in server memory during request processing; analysis responses are marked not to be cached. Closing the app ends its in-memory session, but does not delete reports you export, your photo library or device backups.

服务器短暂处理 IP 地址用于限流；Render 和网络基础设施可能保留连接、安全或故障日志。我们的服务器位于美国，DeepSeek 为中国的服务提供商，处理可能涉及你所在国家以外的地区。我们尚未取得该 API 对本应用终端用户照片的固定保存期限、处理地域或禁止模型训练的专门书面保证，因此不承诺 DeepSeek 对这些请求零保留、即时删除或不用于训练。

The server briefly processes IP addresses for rate limiting; Render and network infrastructure may retain connection, security or error logs. Our server is in the United States and DeepSeek is a provider in China; processing may occur outside your country. We have not obtained a specific written assurance establishing this API’s fixed retention period, processing locations or prohibition on model training for this app’s end-user photos. We therefore do not promise zero retention, immediate deletion or exclusion from training by DeepSeek for these requests.

DeepSeek 的公开隐私政策明确说明，不覆盖开发者下游应用终端用户的数据处理规则；不能将其消费者服务的说明直接当作本应用用户的保存或训练保证。请结合 DeepSeek 开放平台协议、DeepSeek 公开隐私政策及 Render 隐私政策阅读本政策。如不接受上述处理及不确定性，请选择不上传，使用本机手动记录。

DeepSeek’s public privacy policy expressly excludes processing rules for end users of developers’ downstream apps. Its consumer-service statements must not be treated as retention or training guarantees for this app’s users. Read this policy alongside the linked DeepSeek Open Platform Terms, DeepSeek public privacy policy and Render privacy policy. If you do not accept this processing and uncertainty, decline upload and use on-device manual records.

删除或访问请求请联系下方 VITANIA 邮箱。我们会核实请求、处理我们控制的数据，并在需要时向相关提供商寻求协助；不会承诺我们无法核实或控制的第三方删除时间。请不要为删除请求再次发送舌照或密码。

For deletion or access requests, contact VITANIA below. We will verify the request, address data under our control and seek relevant providers’ assistance when needed; we do not promise third-party deletion times we cannot verify or control. Do not resend tongue photos or passwords with a deletion request.

Apple 订阅与使用额度 / Apple subscriptions and usage limits

应用提供无需先购买的三天分析体验：首次获准分析时开始计时，每个 UTC 日期一次成功分析，体验期间最多三次，失败重试另设上限。体验不会自动扣费；结束后由你主动选择是否订阅。Apple 另有符合资格的新订阅用户三天优惠，只有你主动确认 Apple 订阅后，才按购买确认页条款自动续费。可在 Apple 账户订阅设置中管理或取消，并在应用内恢复购买。我们不接收银行卡资料或 Apple 账户密码。

A three-day no-purchase analysis preview begins with the first admitted analysis. It allows one successful analysis per UTC day, up to three overall, with separate retry limits. This preview never charges automatically; afterward you choose whether to subscribe. Apple separately offers eligible new subscribers a three-day introductory offer. Automatic renewal applies only after you explicitly confirm an Apple subscription, under the purchase sheet terms. Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings, or restore purchases in the app. We do not receive payment card details or Apple account passwords.

免费体验使用保存在 iOS 钥匙串或 Android 应用私有存储中的随机凭据。服务器仅保存该凭据的 SHA-256 摘要、体验开始时间、使用次数及短期请求锁，不保存原始凭据、照片或报告。为防止重复开始体验，摘要与开始时间保留至服务停止提供该体验或处理有效删除请求；该假名标识可关联同一设备凭据的分析请求。另保存不包含设备标识的总体服务使用计数，限制免费分析成本。

The preview uses a random credential stored in the iOS Keychain or Android app-private storage. The server stores its SHA-256 hash, preview start time, usage counts and temporary request leases, not the raw credential, photos or reports. To prevent repeated preview enrollment, the hash and start time remain until this preview service is discontinued or a valid deletion request is processed. This pseudonymous identifier can link requests using the same device credential. Aggregate service usage counters without device identifiers also limit free-analysis costs.

应用发送 Apple 签名交易凭据到服务器，用于向 Apple 验证订阅、到期及撤销状态。额度数据库保存按测试或正式环境区分的原始交易编号 SHA-256 摘要、UTC 日期、尝试和成功次数以及短期请求锁；不保存原始交易编号、照片或报告。超过七天的额度记录在后续额度请求时清理。摘要是假名标识，仍可关联同一订阅。Apple 根据其政策保留购买记录。

The app sends Apple-signed transaction proof to our server to verify subscription, expiry and revocation status with Apple. The quota database stores an environment-specific SHA-256 hash of the original transaction identifier, UTC date, attempt and success counts, and a temporary request lease. It does not store raw transaction identifiers, photos or reports. Rows older than seven days are removed on subsequent quota requests. The hash is pseudonymous and can link requests from the same subscription. Apple retains purchase records under its policies.

Data is used for requested analysis, report display, support and service security. This version does not automatically collect user photos into our training dataset. Data may be processed outside Singapore, including in regions where cloud model providers operate. Review provider privacy information before cloud analysis; do not upload if you do not want your photo to leave the device.
