Guanshe Diary · Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-27 · Version 6.10.6

1. 运营者与适用范围 / Operator and scope

本政策适用于 VITANIA PTE. LTD.（新加坡）提供的观舌日记。本版本无需注册，不使用 Supabase 账户服务，订阅由 Apple 管理；应用未集成广告。应用提供舌象观察和健康教育，不提供医学诊断、处方或紧急医疗服务。

This policy covers Guanshe Diary, provided by VITANIA PTE. LTD., Singapore. This version requires no account, uses no Supabase account service, and uses Apple-managed subscriptions without advertising. It provides tongue observations and wellness education, not medical diagnosis, prescriptions or emergency care.

2. 照片与 AI 分析 / Photos and AI analysis

在你勾选上传同意并选择或拍摄照片后，应用将该照片及界面语言发送到我们的分析服务，以生成舌象观察报告。照片可能包含敏感健康信息，请避免包含他人的脸部或身份资料。分析服务可采用 DeepSeek、Anthropic Claude 或本地模型，具体取决于服务配置；使用云端模型时，照片会发送给相应提供商处理。拒绝上传不会阻止你查看一般健康科普或手动填写舌象信息。

After you consent and select or take a photo, the app sends that photo and your interface language to our analysis service to generate an observation report. Photos may reveal sensitive health information; avoid including other people’s faces or identifying details. Depending on service configuration, analysis may use DeepSeek, Anthropic Claude or a local model. Cloud analysis sends the photo to the applicable provider. You may decline upload and still browse general wellness information or enter observations manually.

3. 问卷、本地记录与权限 / Questionnaire, local data and permissions

当前版本的症状选择、问卷和报告显示保存在本次应用会话内，问卷答案不包含在照片分析请求中。界面语言保存在设备本地。应用仅使用你主动选择或拍摄的照片，不读取通讯录、精确位置或健康数据库。系统相机权限可在设备设置中管理。关闭并重新启动应用可结束本次会话；清除网站数据或卸载应用可移除本地偏好，系统备份的处理取决于你的设备设置。

In this version, symptom selections, questionnaire answers and displayed reports are held in the current app session; questionnaire answers are not included in photo-analysis requests. The language preference is stored locally. The app uses only photos you select or take and does not read your address book, precise location or health database. Manage camera permissions in device settings. Fully closing and restarting the app ends the session; clearing website data or uninstalling removes local preferences, subject to your device’s backup settings.

4. 保存期限与服务日志 / Retention and service logs

当前分析接口未建立照片或报告的持久化数据库，也不会主动把照片内容写入应用日志。接口会短暂处理 IP 地址用于请求限流。网络及托管服务可能产生连接、安全或故障日志。云端 AI 提供商可能依其条款与保留设置保存请求内容或服务记录；我们不承诺所有第三方均即时删除或零保留。我们只为提供服务、安全、处理请求及履行法律义务保留必要信息；具体删除请求请联系下方邮箱。

The current analysis endpoint does not create a persistent photo or report database or intentionally write photo contents to application logs. It briefly processes IP addresses for request limiting. Network and hosting services may generate connection, security or error logs. Cloud AI providers may retain request content or service records under their terms and retention settings; we do not promise immediate deletion or zero retention by every third party. We retain information as needed for service delivery, security, requests and legal obligations. Contact the address below for a specific deletion request.

Apple 订阅与使用额度 / Apple subscriptions and usage limits

云端分析需要有效的 Apple 订阅。你需先确认订阅；符合 Apple 优惠资格的用户可免费试用三天，之后按购买确认页的当地价格自动续费，除非在续费前取消。可在 Apple 账户订阅设置中管理或取消，并在应用内恢复购买。我们不接收银行卡资料或 Apple 账户密码。

Cloud analysis requires an active Apple subscription confirmed by you. Eligible users receive a three-day free trial, followed by automatic renewal at the local price shown at purchase unless cancelled before renewal. Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings, or restore purchases in the app. We do not receive payment card details or Apple account passwords.

应用发送 Apple 签名交易凭据到服务器，用于向 Apple 验证订阅、到期及撤销状态。额度数据库保存按测试或正式环境区分的原始交易编号 SHA-256 摘要、UTC 日期、尝试和成功次数以及短期请求锁；不保存原始交易编号、照片或报告。超过七天的额度记录在后续额度请求时清理。摘要是假名标识，仍可关联同一订阅。Apple 根据其政策保留购买记录。

The app sends Apple-signed transaction proof to our server to verify subscription, expiry and revocation status with Apple. The quota database stores an environment-specific SHA-256 hash of the original transaction identifier, UTC date, attempt and success counts, and a temporary request lease. It does not store raw transaction identifiers, photos or reports. Rows older than seven days are removed on subsequent quota requests. The hash is pseudonymous and can link requests from the same subscription. Apple retains purchase records under its policies.

5. 人工联系、下载与朗读 / Human contact, downloads and speech

点击 WhatsApp 或 WeChat 二维码会打开相应外部服务，应用不会自动向联系人发送你的照片、报告或问卷。你主动发送的内容及账户资料受相应平台政策约束，并由接收团队用于答复。下载的文字报告由你自行保管；朗读使用设备或浏览器语音服务，可能受其数据处理设置影响。

WhatsApp and WeChat QR links open external services. The app does not automatically send your photo, report or questionnaire to a contact. Information you choose to send, and your messaging account details, are handled under the platform’s policy and used by the receiving team to respond. You are responsible for downloaded text reports. Report reading uses the device or browser speech service, whose processing depends on its settings.

6. 用途、共享与跨境处理 / Purposes, sharing and international processing

数据用于你请求的分析、报告展示、客户支持及服务安全。当前应用未集成广告或跨应用跟踪功能；我们不会出售健康照片。此版本没有自动收集用户照片用作我们训练数据集的功能。数据可能在新加坡以外、包括云端模型提供商所在地区处理。使用云端分析前，请阅读相关提供商的隐私说明；若不希望照片离开设备，请不要上传。

Data is used for requested analysis, report display, support and service security. This app version has no advertising or cross-app tracking integration; we do not sell health photos. This version does not automatically collect user photos into our training dataset. Data may be processed outside Singapore, including in regions where cloud model providers operate. Review provider privacy information before cloud analysis; do not upload if you do not want your photo to leave the device.

7. 你的选择与联系 / Your choices and contact

你可不上传照片、停止使用云端分析、管理设备权限，并通过 ann.liu@vitania.net 请求了解、更正或删除我们持有的个人资料，或撤回对后续处理的同意。请勿在首次邮件中发送舌照或完整健康记录；我们会先确认请求范围及必要的身份核实。已下载文件和外部聊天记录需在对应设备或服务中管理。

You may decline photos, stop cloud analysis, manage permissions, and contact ann.liu@vitania.net to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or withdraw consent to future processing. Do not include tongue photos or full health records in your first email; we will clarify scope and any necessary identity verification. Manage downloaded files and external chat records in the relevant device or service.

8. 未成年人、安全与更新 / Minors, security and changes

本应用不面向儿童提供独立医疗建议。未成年人应在监护人协助下使用，监护人如发现不适当提交可联系我们。我们使用 HTTPS 传输云端请求，但任何网络或存储方式都无法保证绝对安全。本政策如有实质变更，将更新日期并在适用时重新取得同意。

The app is not intended to provide independent medical advice to children. Minors should use it with a guardian; guardians may contact us about inappropriate submissions. Cloud requests use HTTPS, but no network or storage method is completely secure. Material changes will be reflected in the policy date and, where applicable, renewed consent.
